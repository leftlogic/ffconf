## Community News

We'll miss you next week, but we wanted to highlight the 35th "birthday" of the [first pages published on the web](http://info.cern.ch/hypertext/WWW/TheProject.html) on 6th August 1991.

It's impressive to think it all started there, and now we're part of an industry that connects us in so many ways.

You can get a sense of what that first page looked like through the first browser: [World Wide Web](https://worldwideweb.cern.ch/browser/#http://info.cern.ch/hypertext/WWW/TheProject.html) (what a terrible name!).

Or maybe you're a terminal lover; perhaps the _second_ browser, the [Line Mode browser](https://line-mode.cern.ch/www/hypertext/WWW/TheProject.html) is for you (although, do note that the experience is a little janky; it was written back in 2013!).

Finally, if you want something a little fun, we've been playing [18words](https://18words.com/) a lot lately. I'm sure that can tide you over until we're back!
