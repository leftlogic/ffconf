## Community News

In the month we've been off there's been lots of interesting, fun and scary articles posted. Here's a pick from each of those areas:

- 🤔 Lea Verou sparked conversation across the web about light/dark toggles. Her [latest post covers](https://lea.verou.me/blog/2026/dark-mode-toggles-2/) the full discussions and closes with her own thoughts.
- 🕺 With a focus around music and ownership, Sophie Koonin writes about [reclaiming her music collection](https://localghost.dev/blog/returning-to-owning-things-in-a-streaming-world/) and Jon Hicks used his (beautiful) design skills to make his [perfect personal player](https://hicks.design/journal/jacket-app).
- 😱 404 Media [reported on Amazon slurping](https://www.404media.co/podcast-we-spoke-to-an-amazon-worker-destroying-books-for-ai/) up a vast number of books, scanning them for AI training, then destroying them. So…that's not good.
