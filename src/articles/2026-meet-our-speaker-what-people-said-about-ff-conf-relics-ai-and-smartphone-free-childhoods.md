## Latest Event News

This week we're welcoming Cennydd Bowles to FFConf in our "Meet the Speakers" who will be giving a talk titled: "Revenants".

He'll be asking: What values and visions do we see in today's web? How can we fight for alternatives?

In the interview, we asked: **Twenty years in tech, then a shift to researching what technology should do. What prompted the shift? And is there anything you miss from tech?**

> Partly age: I think many people working in tech need a different plan for the second half of their career. But also I came to the realisation that while I still love technology, the industry turns my stomach.
>
> There are still people fighting the good fight, but I wasn't prepared to be on anyone's team any more, to wear the branded gear at the cost of my own moral silence. And the anti-intellectualism of maker culture had made me feel mentally blunt.
>
> I do miss the money, though.

As Remy ages along with the web industry, he was definitely nodding along reading this!

Read Cennydd's [full interview](https://ffconf.org/articles/2026-meet-the-speaker-cennydd/) and meet him and all **[our speakers on 13th November](https://2026.ffconf.org/)**.
