## Latest Event News

Today we announce our complete line up for FFConf, **only 6 weeks left** to [get your ticket](https://www.tickettailor.com/checkout/view-event/id/8487726/chk/0db6).

Matthew Somerville, creator of traintimes.org.uk finalises our speakers.

**What can you expect when spending the day with us?**

DOOM rebuilt in CSS, an accessible canvas game, 25 years of fast, accessible train times, motion used responsibly, lessons from a chicken restaurant, a greener web, the question of whose utopia the web serves, and **finding purpose in a shaken industry**.

Don't be left with FOMO on 13th November. Join us for FFConf 2026.
